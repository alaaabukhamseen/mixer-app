App setup guide

Your virtual microphone, in three steps

Checking virtual microphone…
  1. 1
    Enable the virtual mic

    The app creates a PipeWire sink and a matching Soundboard Virtual Mic source on your system.

  2. 2
    Pick it in your app

    In your voice app's settings → Voice & Video, set Input Device to Soundboard Virtual Mic. Keep your normal headphones as the output.

  3. 3
    Turn off noise suppression

    Set your app's Noise Suppression to None, otherwise it may swallow your soundboard effects. Keep Hear myself off unless you are testing.

Audio isn’t reaching your app?
